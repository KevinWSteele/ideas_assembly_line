AGENDA ITEM 3: Discussion and vote on proposed protected bike lane for Elm Street.
CHAIR RIVERA: We'll now hear from the public works director on the Elm Street proposal.
PUBLIC WORKS DIRECTOR CHEN: Thank you. The proposal adds a physically separated bike lane on Elm Street between 4th and 9th, removing one lane of street parking on the east side. Staff estimates twelve months for construction, funded from the existing transportation safety budget, no new tax revenue required.
COUNCIL MEMBER NAKAMURA: I've heard from constituents worried about losing parking for the businesses on that block.
COUNCIL MEMBER PATEL: I've also heard strong support from families near the elementary school who want a safer route for kids biking to school.
COUNCIL MEMBER O'BRIEN: Given the safety data Director Chen presented, I'd like to move approval, with a request that staff report back in six months on parking impact.
COUNCIL MEMBER DIAZ: Second.
CHAIR RIVERA: Discussion? Hearing none, all in favor?
CLERK: Diaz, Nakamura, O'Brien, Patel vote aye. Motion carries 4-0. Construction expected to begin June 2026, with a parking impact report due back to council in December 2026.
